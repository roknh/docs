Email Marketing, SMS Brandname, Zalo OA/ZNS — tich hop da kenh trong mot giao dien.

Template Builder

  • Drag & Drop editor — khong can ky nang thiet ke hay HTML

  • Phan tu keo-tha: van ban, hinh anh, nut CTA, layout da cot, HTML tuy chinh

  • Responsive tu dong PC va Mobile

  • Merge Tag bang ky tu #: #Ten, #Ho, #GioiTinh

Deliverability & Loc thong minh

Inbox rate: 90% (tu cong bo)
Bo loc tu dong loai truoc khi gui: email khong ton tai, da unsubscribe, da bounce, da bao spam. Canh bao tuong tac kem (khong mo >20 email) va tan suat cao (>5 email/24h).
  • Cau hinh SPF, DKIM qua DNS (hieu luc sau 12h)
  • IP warming: khuyen nghi 30 ngay cho IP moi
  • Gioi han gui khuyen nghi: toi da 50.000 dia chi/lan

Han che API (Beehexa audit)

  • Khong co API cap nhat contact — chi tao moi

  • Webhook chi tra success/fail, khong tra ket qua chi tiet
  • Callback URL bi khoa cung — 1 URL cho tat ca cong ty
  • Khong co Zapier/Make connector chinh thuc

Khi nao nen chon MISA aiMarketing

  • DN Viet Nam, SME, uu tien ngan sach VND
  • Da dung hoac du dinh dung MISA Ke toan/ERP — tich hop he sinh thai la loi the tuyet doi
  • Can Zalo OA + SMS Viet Nam trong cung nen tang
  • Khong co IT chuyen nghiep, can ho tro tieng Viet
  • Khong can automation qua phuc tap (duoi 25 workflow)

Khi nao nen chon cong cu khac

  • Can A/B testing built-in → Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign
  • Can automation cuc sau voi logic phuc tap → ActiveCampaign, HubSpot
  • Can tich hop API rong voi nhieu he thong ngoai → ActiveCampaign (1.000+)
  • DN >5.000 ban ghi can sync real-time → han che API MISA
  • Can webinar marketing → GetResponse

Danh gia Tong hop

Uu diem, nhuoc diem va khach hang tieu bieu.

Uu diem

  • Ban dia hoa 100%: Zalo OA + ZNS native, SMS 8 nha mang, phap ly VN, tieng Viet, ho tro 24/7
  • He sinh thai tich hop doc: aiMarketing → CRM → Ke toan → HRM
  • Gia canh tranh: Standard ~1,3tr/thang vs HubSpot Pro ~20tr/thang (gap ~15 lan)
  • Smart Fallback: ZNS that bai → tu dong SMS
  • Drag & Drop: khong can ky nang thiet ke, tao email/LP trong vai phut
  • Bo loc email thong minh: inbox rate 90%
  • 20+ bao cao marketing: tu dong dong bo chi phi quang cao tu FB/Google/TikTok Ads

Nhuoc diem

  • Khong co A/B testing native trong email hay workflow
  • Gioi han workflow: chi 25 kich ban o goi Standard
  • API yeu: khong co update contact, timeout >5.000 ban ghi, khong co Zapier/Make
  • Custom domain LP khong self-service — phai lien he ho tro
  • ZNS automation chua ra mat — chi gui thu cong
  • Chi phi leo thang khi dung nhieu module (moi phan he phi rieng)
  • Phu thuoc internet hoan toan (cloud-only)
  • Thoi gian trien khai lau — he sinh thai rong, can dao tao
  • Send Time Optimization va Predictive Analytics chua co built-in
